Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Alfred is an open-source plugin for Claude Code that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what data Alfred accesses, where it is kept, and who it is shared with.

Summary

  • Alfred has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your data.
  • Your profile and Google credentials are stored only on your computer, in files only your user account can read.
  • Alfred contacts other services only to perform what you ask, and sends them only what that request needs.
  • Alfred contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising.

Google user data

With your authorization, Alfred uses these Google permissions:

PermissionUsed to
calendar.eventsRead your events and create, change, or delete them when you ask.
calendar.calendarlist.readonlyList your calendars so you can choose one.
gmail.modifySearch and read your email when you ask, and create labels, label, archive, mark as read, move to trash, or restore messages at your request. Alfred cannot send email or delete it permanently.
tasksRead your task lists and tasks and create, change, complete, or delete them when you ask.

Google data is requested directly from Google's APIs by the copy of Alfred running on your computer and is shown to you in your Claude Code session. Alfred does not store your events, tasks, or email; it keeps only the OAuth token, on your computer, so it can reach your account again.

Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train artificial-intelligence models, and not shared with anyone except as described in this policy.

Claude

Alfred is used through Claude Code. When Alfred reads your calendar, tasks, profile, or weather to answer you, that information becomes part of your conversation with Claude and is processed by Anthropic according to Anthropic's privacy policy and the terms of your Claude plan.

Data stored on your computer

DataPurpose
Your profile: name, family members and their birthdays, places, vehicles, habits, and preferences you chose to sharePersonalize answers and reports
Google OAuth client file and tokenConnect to your Google account

These files live in Claude Code's data directory for the plugin. Alfred saves a profile entry only after you confirm it.

Other services

  • Open-Meteo (weather): receives the name or coordinates of a town, never your name or street address.
  • TomTom (traffic and travel times): receives the coordinates of the trips you ask about and, when a place has no saved coordinates yet, the address to look up; never your name.
  • Osservatorio prezzi carburanti (MIMIT) (fuel prices): receives the coordinates of the area to search and the ids of the stations you ask about, never your name or address.
  • OpenStreetMap (address lookup): receives an address only when TomTom does not know the street, never your name.

Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Ask Alfred "what do you know about me?" to see your profile, and "forget …" to delete any item.
  • Revoke Alfred's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the plugin deletes its data directory, unless you choose to keep it.

Children

Alfred is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's history on GitHub, with a new "Last updated" date.
