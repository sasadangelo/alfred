Alfred

A personal-assistant plugin for Claude Code.

Alfred connects Claude Code to your Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Gmail, gives weather forecasts, travel times with live traffic and fuel prices, and keeps a local profile of you (family, places, habits) so every answer fits your life. Your personal data stays on your own computer.

What it does

FeatureAsk things like
Google Calendar"What do I have this week?", "Add the dentist on Friday at 10"
Google Tasks"What do I have to do this weekend?", "Add 'paint the garage' to the Home list"
Gmail"What unread email do I have today?", "Label the OVH emails OVHCloud", "Trash the promotions older than 3 months"
Weather"How should I dress for Sunday's run?", "Do I need an umbrella for the office?"
Traffic"How is the traffic to the office?", "When should I leave to be at the dentist at 10?"
Fuel"Where is petrol cheapest near home?", "Prices at my saved stations"
Your profile"Remember my wife's birthday", "What do you know about me?"

Install

In Claude Code, with uv installed:

/plugin marketplace add sasadangelo/alfred
/plugin install alfred@sasadangelo

To use Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Gmail, create your own OAuth client in Google Cloud Console and authorize Alfred: the plugin guides you step by step.

Privacy

Alfred runs entirely on your computer and has no servers of its own. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
